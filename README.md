## About me

George is a DevOps, Cloud Platform Engineer with a systems-thinking mindset and a strong obsession with efficiency, simplicity, and optimization.

He enjoys turning fragmented workflows, disconnected systems, and repetitive manual processes into holistic, intelligent, and reliable systems.

His approach sits at the intersection of automation, AI, data, and infrastructure. He uses Cloud/DevOps principles to make solutions secure, reliable, maintainable, and scalable.

He is particularly interested in the space where design meets technology and engineering — creating systems that are technically robust underneath while remaining simple, intuitive, and effective for the people using them.
